**Teacher:** Mufti Umar Aejaz

## Introduction

Continuing the **method of Hajj**, this lesson covers the **8th of Dhū al-Ḥijjah (Yawm al-Tarwiyah)** at **Minā**, the move to **ʿArafāt** on the **9th**, the **Wuqūf (وقوف)** [standing] and its permitted area, the **Ghusl** of ʿArafah, the two **Khuṭbahs**, and — in detail — the **Ḥanafī jurisprudence of combining Ẓuhr and ʿAṣr (Jamʿ bayn al-Ṣalātayn)** at ʿArafāt: its conditions, the congregation requirement, and the rulings for resident vs. traveller imams.

---

## Part 1 — The 8th of Dhū al-Ḥijjah (Yawm al-Tarwiyah): Minā

- **Movement:** On the **8th (Yawm al-Tarwiyah / يوم التروية)**, pilgrims move to **Minā (منى)**.
- **The five prayers at Minā (Sunnah):** the pilgrim performs **five prayers** there — **Ẓuhr, ʿAṣr, Maghrib, ʿIshā'**, and the **Fajr of the 9th** — each **at its own prescribed time** (not combined).
- **Historical basis:** this follows the example of **Ibrāhīm عليه السلام**, whom **Jibrā'īl عليه السلام** led in these five prayers at Minā after taking him there on the Day of Tarwiyah.

---

## Part 2 — The 9th of Dhū al-Ḥijjah: Moving to ʿArafāt

- **Departure:** after the **Fajr** prayer at Minā on the **9th**, the pilgrim waits until **sunrise**, then proceeds to **ʿArafāt (عرفات)**.
- **Where to stand (Wuqūf):** a pilgrim may stand **anywhere within the boundaries of ʿArafāt except Wādī ʿUranah (وادي عُرَنة)** — the Valley of ʿUranah.

> **«عَرَفَاتُ كُلُّهَا مَوْقِفٌ، وَارْتَفِعُوا عَنْ بَطْنِ عُرَنَةَ»**
>
> *"All of ʿArafāt is a place of standing, but keep away from the bottom of ʿUranah."*
>
> **Ref:** Sunan Ibn Mājah, 3012 (also narrated in Abū Dāwūd).

- **Ghusl of ʿArafah:** it is **Mustaḥabb (recommended)** to make **ghusl** on the day of ʿArafah — for **cleanliness** and to improve the atmosphere of the massive gathering, similar to the ghusl for **Jumuʿah** or **ʿĪd**.

---

## Part 3 — The Khuṭbah and Adhān

- **Timing:** once the sun begins to decline (**Zawāl / زوال**), the imam delivers **two khuṭbahs** from the **minbar**.
- **Purpose:** the primary aim is to **teach the pilgrims the manāsik (مناسك)** [rituals] of Hajj.
- **Sequence (preferred view):** the imam **ascends the minbar first**, and the **adhān** is called while he is upon it.
- **Its status — Sunnah (not a condition of the prayer):** unlike the **Jumuʿah** khuṭbah — which is **farḍ** for the validity of the prayer — the **ʿArafah khuṭbah is Sunnah**. The **Ẓuhr and ʿAṣr remain valid** even if the khuṭbah is not delivered, though **omitting it without reason is wrong**, as it opposes the Prophet's ﷺ Sunnah.
- **Content:** the sermon should **begin with the Takbīr** and include teaching the specific rulings of Hajj.

> **«اللهُ أَكْبَرُ اللهُ أَكْبَرُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَاللهُ أَكْبَرُ اللهُ أَكْبَرُ وَلِلَّهِ الْحَمْدُ»**
>
> *"Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest; there is no deity but Allah; Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest, and to Allah belongs all praise."*

---

## Part 4 — Combining Ẓuhr and ʿAṣr (Jamʿ bayn al-Ṣalātayn)

At ʿArafāt, **Ẓuhr and ʿAṣr** are performed together **in the time of Ẓuhr** (**Jamʿ Taqdīm / جمع تقديم**).

**Procedure:**

- **One adhān** for both prayers.
- **Two separate iqāmahs** (one for Ẓuhr, one for ʿAṣr).
- **No Sunnah or Nawāfil** are prayed **between** the two farḍ prayers — to avoid any break (faṣl). If a **significant break** occurs, the Sunnah of combining is missed, and the default (a **separate adhān** for each prayer) would technically apply.

**Recitation style:** performed **silently (sirrī)**, exactly like ordinary Ẓuhr and ʿAṣr — **not** aloud like Jumuʿah or ʿĪd — because the primary purpose of the ʿArafāt gathering is **Duʿā' and Wuqūf**, not the prayer itself.

---

## Part 5 — Ḥanafī Jurisprudence of the Combined Prayer

The Ḥanafī school treats this combining as an **exceptional case**, established **strictly on the Prophet's ﷺ practice**.

### Conditions for combining

1. **Order:** **Ẓuhr must be performed first**, then **ʿAṣr**.
2. **A valid Ẓuhr first:** the ʿAṣr must follow a **valid Ẓuhr** — one performed **after the time of Zawāl has genuinely entered**.

### The congregation (jamāʿah) requirement

According to the Ḥanafīs, this combination is **only for those praying with the imam** in the main congregation.

| Situation | Ruling |
|-----------|--------|
| **Praying with the imam** (main jamāʿah) | May **combine** Ẓuhr & ʿAṣr |
| **Praying alone** (in one's tent or elsewhere) | Must pray **each at its own time** — Ẓuhr at Ẓuhr time, ʿAṣr at ʿAṣr time |
| **A small jamāʿah** formed (even in a tent) | **May combine** — they follow the ruling of combining |

---

## Part 6 — Resident vs. Traveller Imams

| Imam | Ẓuhr & ʿAṣr |
|------|-------------|
| **Resident (of Makkah)** | Prays the **full four rakʿahs** each |
| **Traveller (Musāfir)** | Prays **two rakʿahs (Qaṣr)** each |

**The traveller imam,** after finishing, instructs the residents behind him:

> **«أَتِمُّوا صَلَاتَكُمْ يَا أَهْلَ مَكَّةَ فَإِنَّا قَوْمٌ سَفْرٌ»**
>
> *"Complete your prayer, O people of Makkah, for we are a travelling people."*
>
> *(Based on the Prophet's ﷺ general practice when leading residents as a traveller — as cited in class.)*

- **Followers who are travellers** pray **two rakʿahs** behind him.
- **Followers who are residents** must **stand and complete the remaining two rakʿahs** on their own after the imam concludes.
