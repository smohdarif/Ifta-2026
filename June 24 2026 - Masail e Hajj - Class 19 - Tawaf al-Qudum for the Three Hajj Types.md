## Introduction

This lesson clarifies **Ṭawāf al-Qudūm (طواف القدوم)** [the arrival ṭawāf] for each of the three types of Hajj — **Tamattuʿ (تمتّع)**, **Ifrād (إفراد)**, and **Qirān (قِران)** — establishing that the **Mutamattiʿ** does **not** make a separate arrival ṭawāf/saʿī for Hajj, while the **Mufrid** and **Qārin** do. It then discusses the **relative merit (afḍaliyyah)** of nāfil ṭawāf vs. nāfil ṣalāh (differing for outsiders vs. Makkans), and the ruling that **two rakʿahs** should follow **each set of seven rounds (usbūʿ)** — with a differing view of **Imam Abū Yūsuf** to be taken up next class.

## Part 1 — Ṭawāf al-Qudūm for the Mutamattiʿ

### The preferred opinion

For the **Mutamattiʿ**, there is **no recommendation** to make a separate ṭawāf and saʿī (i.e. Ṭawāf al-Qudūm) for the sake of Hajj before departing for Minā:

- He performs **one ṭawāf and saʿī — for his ʿUmrah**. When done, he is **done**.
- He then proceeds to perform the **Hajj**: later he does the **ṭawāf (al-Ziyārah)** and makes his **Hajj saʿī after it**.
- So it is **not** recommended for him to make any ṭawāf besides the ʿUmrah ṭawāf, or any saʿī besides the ʿUmrah saʿī, **before the acts of Hajj**.

### The non-preferred opinion (analogy to Ifrād & Qirān)

One view (**not preferred**) compares Tamattuʿ to **Ifrād and Qirān** — where Ṭawāf al-Qudūm **is** recommended (and a saʿī may optionally follow it). This view says Ṭawāf al-Qudūm should likewise be **recommended for the Mutamattiʿ** — a ṭawāf **for the sake of Hajj** (his earlier ṭawāf having been for ʿUmrah).

### The counter (why the preferred view holds)

In **Ifrād and Qirān**, Ṭawāf al-Qudūm is sunnah/recommended **because the person arrives in Makkah with the intention of Hajj**:
- **Ifrād:** intention of **Hajj only**.
- **Qirān:** a **combination** — intention of both **ʿUmrah and Hajj**.

But the **Mutamattiʿ does not arrive in Makkah with the intention of Hajj** — at arrival his intention was **ʿUmrah only**. Therefore there is **no Ṭawāf al-Qudūm for the sake of Hajj** for him.

### A further (non-preferred) view — assuming Iḥrām on the 8th

Another opinion (attributed to al-Ḥasan): if the Mutamattiʿ **assumes iḥrām for Hajj on the Day of Tarwiyah (8th Dhū al-Ḥijjah) before Zawāl**, he **may** make a Ṭawāf al-Qudūm with saʿī for Hajj as well. But if he assumes iḥrām **after Zawāl** on the 8th, he must **depart for Minā** and will **not** occupy himself with any (further) ṭawāf or saʿī. **This too is not the preferred opinion.** The preferred opinion remains: the Mutamattiʿ completes his ʿUmrah, then goes to Minā, then performs the remaining acts of Hajj.

## Part 2 — Mufrid and Qārin (and Their Extra Nāfil Ṭawāf)

The **Mufrid** and **Qārin** complete their **Ṭawāf al-Qudūm and (Hajj) Saʿī** performed for the sake of Hajj, and **remain in iḥrām**. Before departing for Minā (on the Day of Tarwiyah), they **may perform additional (nāfil) ṭawāf** — and, if they are **outsiders (Āfāqī)**, **as much as possible**.

### Nāfil Ṭawāf vs. Nāfil Ṣalāh — the merit varies

| Person | Which nāfil is better? | Why |
|--------|------------------------|-----|
| **Outsider (Āfāqī)** | **Nāfil Ṭawāf** is better than nāfil ṣalāh | He **will not get this chance again** — ṭawāf is available only here |
| **Makkan (resident)** | **Nāfil Ṣalāh** is better than nāfil ṭawāf | Ṣalāh is among the greatest acts in Islam; the resident **has ongoing access** to ṭawāf |

**Principle:** the **afḍaliyyah (superiority)** of an act **varies** from time to time and from person to person.

> **Illustrative analogy — Ribāṭ:** a man guarding the frontier (ribāṭ) in the way of Allah, protecting the Muslims, while also wishing to engage in dhikr/ʿibādah. Which is better — **holding his post** or **occupying himself with ʿibādah**? It **varies with the situation**: if **someone else** can take his post, it is better for him to engage in ʿibādah; but if **no one else** is there, it is better for him to **guard and protect** the Muslim army.

### Manner of the extra nāfil ṭawāf

For these **additional** ṭawāfs (beyond the Ṭawāf al-Qudūm), he:

- does **not** perform **Ramal** — he walks **normally**;
- does **not** perform a **saʿī** after them — the **only** saʿī is the **first one**, done for Hajj alongside the Ṭawāf al-Qudūm.

## Part 3 — Two Rakʿahs After Each Usbūʿ (Set of Seven Rounds)

For each **usbūʿ (أسبوع)** [a set of seven rounds of ṭawāf], he performs the **two rakʿahs** of ṭawāf.

- It is **disliked (makrūh)** to **combine two usbūʿ (two complete ṭawāfs) without the two rakʿahs in between** them.
- So however many ṭawāfs he performs during his stay — whether the count is **odd or even** — he prays **two rakʿahs after each set of seven**, and does not string two sets together without them.

**A debatable point:** **Imam Abū Yūsuf** holds a **different opinion** here, arguing from a **ḥadīth**. This is a somewhat contested case that will be discussed in a **separate class** (the teacher had to leave early due to an emergency).

## Closing

- **Covered:** the **Mutamattiʿ** makes no separate Ṭawāf al-Qudūm for Hajj (his ṭawāf/saʿī were for ʿUmrah; the Hajj saʿī follows Ṭawāf al-Ziyārah) — with the non-preferred analogy to Ifrād/Qirān and its rebuttal (intention at arrival), and al-Ḥasan's non-preferred 8th-of-Dhū-al-Ḥijjah view; the **Mufrid and Qārin** perform Ṭawāf al-Qudūm + Hajj saʿī and may add nāfil ṭawāf; **nāfil ṭawāf is better for the outsider**, **nāfil ṣalāh for the Makkan** (afḍaliyyah varies — the ribāṭ analogy); extra nāfil ṭawāfs are **without Ramal and without a following saʿī**; and **two rakʿahs after each usbūʿ** (makrūh to combine two sets without them).
